About the role We are seeking a full-time cleaner-porter, on a fixed term contract to cover maternity, to join our friendly, supportive, and professional Campus Services team. No two days are the same in this varied and rewarding role. Working as part of a dedicated team, you will contribute to the smooth day-to-day operation of our campus by undertaking a range of cleaning and portering duties, including: Maintaining cleanliness and tidiness within designated areas Emptying general waste and recycling bins Assisting with the movement of furniture, equipment, and deliveries Supporting room set
